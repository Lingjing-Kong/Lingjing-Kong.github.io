Welcome! I joined the NYU Center for Data Science in 2026. Before that, I did my Ph.D. in Computer Science at Carnegie Mellon University, where I was fortunate to be advised by Yuejie Chi, Eric P. Xing, and Kun Zhang.

I am curious about what it takes for AI to go beyond prediction: to understand the concepts and mechanisms behind the data, and not only the patterns in it. This is what I study under the name causal representation learning.

Research

My work connects causality and machine learning in both directions:

  • Causality for machine learning: causal principles for models that transfer to new environments, are interpretable, and can be controlled precisely.
  • Machine learning for causality: scalable learning methods for causal discovery and inference, with scientific discovery as the testing ground.
