Welcome! I joined the [NYU Center for Data Science](https://cds.nyu.edu/) in 2026. Before that, I did my Ph.D. in Computer Science at Carnegie Mellon University, where I was fortunate to be advised by [Yuejie Chi](https://yuejiechi.github.io/), [Eric P. Xing](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~epxing/), and [Kun Zhang](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/kunz1/).

I am curious about what it takes for AI to go **beyond prediction**: to understand the concepts and mechanisms behind the data, and not only the patterns in it. This is what I study under the name **causal representation learning**.

## Research

My work connects causality and machine learning in both directions:

- **Causality for machine learning:** causal principles for models that [transfer](/research/#transfer) to new environments, are [interpretable](/research/#interpret), and can be [controlled](/research/#control) precisely.
- **Machine learning for causality:** scalable learning methods for [causal discovery and inference](/research/#ml-for-causality), with scientific discovery as the testing ground.
